Meet your harmonium

An experimental web version. Each key plays one note of the sargam scale.
Rock your MacBook's lid back and forth to pump air, like bellows
You can also drag the bellows with your mouse and click the keys.
🔐

Allow access to the lid sensor

To pump with your lid, this page needs permission to read your
MacBook's lid-angle sensor. That's all it reads, and it stays in your browser.
Click Allow below. Your browser will show a device list with one item in it: select that item, then click Connect.
Press A S D F G H J to play · pump air by moving your MacBook lid, or drag the bellows · get the Mac app
